Our Story

From the Banquet Hall Kitchen, to Your Private Table

Chef Dongli Lin's culinary education began before dawn, in the kitchens of Taiwan's grand banquet halls — watching senior chefs prepare ingredients in the small hours, watching teams serve hundreds of guests with precision, and learning how much unseen discipline sits behind every table that looks effortless.

That apprenticeship taught him something a menu alone cannot: a great meal is never just food served well. A chairman's invitation is an expression of trust. A corporate gathering is an extension of a brand. A family banquet carries the weight of everyone at the table. This is SHUYUAN's foundation — the discipline of Taiwan's traditional banquet culture, refined through the lens of fine dining, shaped into a private dining language built for Taiwan's most important occasions.

About Us

What is Private Dining?

Private dining brings a fine dining restaurant's experience directly to a place of your choosing — your home, your office, or a private venue. Our chefs arrive with their own ingredients and equipment, and cook on site. Compared to dining out, it offers absolute privacy, a fully customized menu, and an uninterrupted atmosphere built entirely around your guests.

SHUYUAN Culinary Culture specializes in high-stakes occasions: chairman receptions, corporate VIP hospitality, private residence banquets, milestone celebrations, proposals, and brand launch events. Whether it's an intimate dinner for 6 or a 120-guest corporate year-end banquet, we deliver an end-to-end experience.

We believe food is not only about taste — it's a connection. From menu design and ingredient selection to live cooking, plating, and post-event cleanup, every step is held to the same standard. Chef Lin brings over 20 years in the industry and Taiwan's Golden Hat culinary award, leading a team of accomplished chefs into every event.
